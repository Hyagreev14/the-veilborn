# The Open Expanse — Mechanics

> **Status:** Gameplay role and fairness principles are defined; specific mechanics remain provisional.

## Primary gameplay role: route reading

The Open Expanse should make the player think about **where to go**, not constantly about whether the ground rules have changed.

Players should be able to:
- Read the broad terrain and identify likely destinations.
- Compare a direct crossing with a longer, more secure route.
- Use distant silhouettes and island shapes to maintain a sense of direction.
- Pause, observe, and choose their next move without being pressured by a constant hazard.

The biome should reward attention and route planning without requiring pixel-perfect jumps or a single hidden path.

## Gravity

Gravity should generally be stable and predictable in this biome. The Open Expanse serves as a useful baseline against which more unusual gravity regions can feel distinct.

No special gravity ability, gravity cycle, or random force is confirmed for this biome. If a localized anomaly is added later, it must be clearly signaled, learnable, and fair, and must follow [dimension-wide mechanics](../../dimension/mechanics.md).

## Traversal and environmental hazards

The gaps between landmasses are a natural exploration challenge, but they should not become a constant punishment loop.

- Routes should usually offer readable landing areas or visible alternatives.
- Avoid unavoidable falls caused by hidden terrain or unreadable effects.
- Do not assume a specific Veilborn item is mandatory for basic traversal.
- Optional shortcuts may demand more skill than the safer route.
- Hazards, if introduced, need clear visual or audio cues and a reasonable response.

A special wind, stamina, fall-damage, or weather system is not part of the confirmed design.

## Exploration and discoveries

Optional landmarks and small discoveries may reward players for looking beyond the obvious route. The biome should not depend on a single puzzle or mandatory structure to be worthwhile.

Clues can suggest that other regions follow different rules, but this biome should not explain the dimension's entire history or reveal protected mysteries.

## Resources and progression

No biome-exclusive resource or equipment gate is confirmed. Ore generation belongs to the dimension-wide progression plan and should not be invented here.

The Open Expanse should be viable to explore with sensible vanilla equipment. Veilcryst may later make some routes or environmental interactions more manageable, but it must not be required for every ordinary crossing.

## Multiplayer and persistence

Any future mechanics must behave consistently for players sharing a world. Temporary effects should resolve predictably, and the biome must not punish players merely for logging out or leaving the area.

## Open decisions

- Whether route landmarks provide any explicit navigation benefit.
- Whether the biome has a small, optional environmental interaction.
- Whether any local hazard adds enough value to justify its complexity.
- Which discoveries are natural features versus generated points of interest.
