# The Open Expanse — General Design

> **Status:** Biome name and broad identity agreed. This design establishes a coherent direction; exact generation, assets, and implementation remain provisional.

## Core identity

A vast, open region of floating landscapes where broad horizons, separated landmasses, and distant silhouettes make the dimension feel immense.

The Open Expanse should deliver the first clear feeling of **space to explore**: beautiful, quiet, and inviting, with just enough strangeness to make the distance feel unfamiliar.

## Purpose in the dimension

The Open Expanse is the dimension's **open-navigation reference biome**. Its broad terrain and long sightlines let players read the landscape, choose routes, spot distant points of interest, and gradually learn the scale of The Veilborn.

It is not automatically the dimension's entrance biome; the secret entry method and exact progression order remain intentionally undefined.

## Signature features

- **Broad floating landmasses:** More room to move and look around than in tightly fragmented regions.
- **Long sightlines:** Distant islands and natural silhouettes make the world feel connected even when routes between them are not obvious.
- **Open-sky gaps:** The spaces between islands are part of the landscape and encourage deliberate route planning.
- **Natural horizon landmarks:** Distinctive rock forms or island silhouettes may help players orient themselves without turning the biome into a ruin field.
- **A mostly legible baseline:** Gravity and traversal should generally be predictable here, making unusual regions elsewhere feel meaningfully different.

These are design directions, not a locked feature checklist.

## Intended player experience

1. **Look outward:** Notice far-off islands, silhouettes, and possible routes.
2. **Choose a path:** Decide whether to follow a broad landmass, cross a gap, or investigate a landmark.
3. **Explore at the player's pace:** Find small discoveries and optional points of interest without constant pressure.
4. **Notice the strange:** Subtle environmental inconsistencies can hint that the dimension is not entirely ordinary, without competing with the more specialized biomes.

The biome should reward curiosity and orientation, not require a single correct route.

## Distinction from other biomes

- The Open Expanse emphasizes **breadth, visibility, and navigation**.
- The Shattered Heights emphasizes fractured high terrain and more complex vertical routes.
- The Gravity Fracture emphasizes conspicuous disruption of gravity.
- The Fallen Monuments emphasizes large-scale ancient architecture.
- The Silver Sea emphasizes the dimension's mysterious silver ocean.

These distinctions are role guidelines, not rigid borders. A distant ruin or unusual gravity event could appear at a boundary, but should not erase the biome's primary identity.

## Resources and progression

No unique block, ore placement, creature, structure, or equipment requirement is finalized for this biome. Veilcryst and Aetherium generation must be decided as part of the wider progression design, not assumed from this page.

The biome should remain explorable without requiring a specific Veilborn equipment set.

## Open decisions

- Final terrain palette, vegetation, and natural landmark language.
- How frequent and wide the open gaps should be.
- Whether the biome has a distinctive ambient environmental effect.
- Which small points of interest, if any, belong here.
- Its distribution, size, and transitions to neighboring biomes.
