# Veilcryst — Ore and Material Design

> **Status:** Name selected. Appearance, generation, processing, equipment, and exact mechanics remain provisional.

Veilcryst is the first new ore/material tier planned for The Veilborn beyond vanilla Diamond. It takes the place of the earlier working name “Veilstone” for the first progression material; Veilstone now refers only to the dimension's stone family.

## Design identity

Veilcryst should help players adapt to The Veilborn's unusual terrain and environmental phenomena.

- **Progression role:** Early-to-mid dimension progression.
- **Specialization:** Traversal and situational environmental utility.
- **Relative strength:** Intended to exceed Diamond in selected roles, not to make every vanilla item obsolete.
- **Acquisition:** Exploration and mining, followed by a familiar refining or crafting process, potentially with a special ingredient.
- **Equipment scope:** A focused selection of useful items, not necessarily a complete set.

## Visual direction

A cool, crystalline mineral with restrained silver or pale-blue highlights is a possible direction. The ore should be recognizable against Veilstone and should not look identical to Aetherium. This is not a finalized texture specification.

## Discovery and processing

Mining should feel understandable to Minecraft players. Deposits should reward exploration while remaining reasonably discoverable so progression does not depend on extreme luck. A basic refining process may include a distinctive ingredient if it adds meaning without becoming busywork.

Exact dimensions, vein sizes, generation frequency, drops, recipes, and tool requirements have not been decided.

## Gameplay direction

Veilcryst equipment may support movement control, situational environmental protection, and selected interactions with ancient systems. These are design candidates, not confirmed item abilities. Exact items, stats, and interactions remain open.

## Scope boundary

Veilcryst and Aetherium are the only two new ore/material tiers currently planned beyond vanilla Diamond. Additional crafting components or stations do not count as new ore tiers.

## Open decisions

- Ore and refined-material appearances
- Generation locations and frequency
- Processing recipe and any special ingredient
- Which equipment pieces are worth making
- Exact traversal, protection, and interaction effects
