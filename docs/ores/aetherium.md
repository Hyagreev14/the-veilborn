# Aetherium — Ore and Material Design

> **Status:** Provisional design. The name is retained as the final planned new ore/material tier; generation, refining, equipment, and mechanics may change. Planned features are not guarantees.

Aetherium is the final planned new ore/material tier in The Veilborn. It should give players who already have strong vanilla equipment a meaningful reason to explore the dimension's rarer and more challenging regions.

This document describes gameplay direction rather than fixed statistics, recipes, or implementation details.

## 1. Design identity

- **Role:** Advanced material for selected specialist equipment.
- **Progression position:** Intended to surpass Netherite in the specific equipment roles it supports, without relying on indiscriminate raw-stat increases.
- **Acquisition:** A combination of rare mining, major progression achievements, and additional crafting components.
- **Refining:** A reusable ancient refining station discovered or restored during progression.
- **Equipment scope:** A balanced selection of specialist items, not necessarily a complete set of every armor piece, weapon, and tool.
- **Scope:** One of exactly two new ore/material tiers currently planned beyond vanilla Diamond. Veilcryst is the other.

Aetherium should be valuable because it unlocks meaningful options, not only because it has a larger number attached to it.

## 2. Appearance and atmosphere

The current visual direction is a dark, metallic crystal with restrained silver and violet or pale iridescent highlights. It should stand out from ordinary stone without becoming a bright beacon visible from everywhere.

Possible visual cues:
- Dark mineral faces with fine luminous fractures.
- A subtle reflective or shifting highlight.
- A refined material that looks cleaner and more deliberate than the raw ore.
- Stronger visual distinction between raw ore, refined material, and crafted equipment.

These are visual ideas, not final texture specifications. The design should remain readable in Minecraft's block-based visual style.

## 3. Discovery and world placement

Aetherium should be harder to obtain than Veilcryst. Deposits may appear in rare or difficult-to-access regions, potentially near ancient structures or other important exploration sites.

Design goals:
- Finding a deposit should feel like a meaningful discovery.
- Access should be challenging for a reason, not merely tedious.
- Clues and landmarks may help observant players infer where to explore.
- Deposits should be rare without making progression rely on unreasonable luck.
- Large quantities should not be available immediately upon entering the dimension.

The exact biome list, height range, vein size, generation frequency, and mining requirements remain unresolved. The method of entering The Veilborn remains intentionally undisclosed.

## 4. Obtaining and refining

Aetherium's planned acquisition loop combines three activities:

1. **Explore and mine:** Find a rare deposit and collect its ore using an appropriate tool.
2. **Earn progression components:** Complete relevant exploration milestones or major encounters to obtain a separate crafting component or unlock a needed capability. Exact encounters and rewards remain undecided.
3. **Refine at an ancient station:** Use a reusable ancient refining station to process the ore into a usable material.

The refining station should be discovered or restored as part of progression. Restoring it may visibly change the structure or nearby environment if that creates a clear and satisfying payoff.

Refining principles:
- The process should be understandable once discovered.
- Each step should serve exploration, mastery, or world restoration.
- Rare components should come from meaningful gameplay rather than repetitive grinding.
- The station should ideally remain usable after restoration instead of being consumed by one crafting action.
- Exact recipes, fuel or energy requirements, processing time, and station interactions are open questions.

## 5. Equipment role

Aetherium is intended for a balanced selection of specialized equipment, not automatically a complete set of every possible item.

Potential categories include:
- **Weapons:** Advanced combat options with a distinctive tactical ability.
- **Armor pieces:** Situational defense or utility against demanding threats.
- **Tools:** Only where a tool offers a genuinely useful gathering or interaction benefit.

Not every category must be included. Each item should earn its place through a clear purpose and should be balanced against the rest of Minecraft.

### Ability philosophy

Potential abilities should be distinctive and situational rather than universal upgrades that invalidate other equipment. For example, a weapon might offer a tactical effect under a particular condition, or an armor piece might help with a specific class of challenge.

These are design directions only. No exact ability, recipe, item list, or stat value is finalized.

## 6. Balance and progression

Aetherium is intended to exceed Netherite in its chosen equipment roles, but it should not trivialize every vanilla encounter or remove the need to make equipment choices.

- Compare damage, armor, durability, mining speed, and other values against relevant vanilla items before setting numbers.
- Avoid enormous, automatic power jumps between materials.
- Make rarity and crafting requirements proportionate to the benefit.
- Preserve the usefulness of Netherite for players who have not progressed deeply into The Veilborn.
- Avoid requiring players to repeatedly farm a single encounter just to replace ordinary equipment.
- Prefer meaningful abilities and trade-offs over raw numerical inflation.

Exact balance values should remain open until the equipment list and gameplay needs are better understood.

## 7. Relationship to other systems

Aetherium may connect to:
- **Biomes and exploration:** Rare deposits can encourage players to visit different regions.
- **Major encounters:** Encounters may award components or unlock refining capabilities without simply replacing the mining step.
- **Ancient structures:** A restored station can provide a tangible world change and a lasting crafting location.
- **Equipment progression:** Aetherium offers specialized options rather than a mandatory upgrade for every slot.
- **Dimension mastery:** Its acquisition should reflect the player's growing ability to navigate and understand The Veilborn.

These connections should support one coherent progression loop. They do not define the dimension's full lore or the identity of any unrevealed boss.

## 8. Open questions

- Which regions can contain Aetherium Ore?
- How rare should deposits be, and what mining requirements should apply?
- What component is earned through progression, and from what type of activity?
- How is the ancient refining station discovered and restored?
- Which specific equipment items are worth crafting from Aetherium?
- What abilities make those items distinctive while remaining balanced?
- How should the station and its surroundings visibly change when restored?

These questions should be answered gradually. This page is a starting point, not a claim that Aetherium or its mechanics have been implemented.

---

**Guiding principle:** Aetherium should feel earned through exploration, mastery, and discovery—an advanced material that expands the player's choices without turning The Veilborn into a simple grind for bigger numbers.
