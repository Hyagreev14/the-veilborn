# Aetherium — Ore and Material Design

> **Status:** Provisional design. The name is retained as the final planned new ore/material tier; generation, refining, equipment, and mechanics may change.

Aetherium is the final planned new ore/material tier in The Veilborn. It is intended to give players who already have strong vanilla equipment a meaningful reason to explore rarer and more challenging regions.

## Design identity

- **Role:** Advanced material for selected specialist equipment.
- **Progression position:** Intended to surpass Netherite in the specific equipment roles it supports, without relying on indiscriminate raw-stat increases.
- **Acquisition:** A combination of rare mining, major progression achievements, and additional crafting components.
- **Refining:** A reusable ancient refining station discovered or restored during progression.
- **Equipment scope:** A balanced selection of specialist items, not necessarily a complete set of every armor piece, weapon, and tool.

## Visual direction

The current direction is a dark, metallic crystal with restrained silver and violet or pale iridescent highlights. The ore should stand out from ordinary stone without becoming a beacon visible from everywhere. These are visual ideas, not final texture requirements.

## Discovery and acquisition

Aetherium should be harder to obtain than Veilcryst. Deposits may appear in rare or difficult-to-access regions, potentially near ancient structures or major exploration sites.

Design goals:

- Discovery should feel significant.
- Access should be challenging for a reason, not merely tedious.
- Landmarks and clues may help observant players decide where to explore.
- Progression should not depend on unreasonable luck.
- Large quantities should not be available immediately upon entering the dimension.

Exact biome locations, height ranges, vein sizes, generation frequency, mining requirements, drops, and recipes remain undecided. The dimension's secret entry method and unrevealed boss lore must remain undisclosed here.

## Refining and equipment

The ancient refining station should be reusable and tied to discovery or restoration. Its exact recipe and operation are open. Aetherium equipment should offer carefully chosen specialist capabilities, with exact items and balance decided separately.

## Scope boundary

Aetherium is the final planned new ore/material tier. Veilcryst is the only other new tier currently planned beyond vanilla Diamond.

## Open decisions

- Ore generation and required progression milestones
- Refining station discovery, restoration, and recipe
- Required crafting components
- Selected equipment and specialist abilities
- Balance against Netherite and multiplayer fairness
