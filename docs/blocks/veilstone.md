# Veilstone — Stone Family

> **Status:** The name and role are selected; appearance, exact variants, generation, and implementation remain provisional.

Veilstone is the dimension's stone family. It is used as natural terrain and may also appear in crafted construction blocks and ancient architecture.

## Identity

Veilstone should feel native to The Veilborn: pale, cool, mineral, and ancient-looking, while remaining readable in Minecraft's block-based visual style. It should be distinct from ordinary Overworld stone without relying on excessive glow.

Possible visual directions include muted grey or silver tones, restrained pale-blue accents, and subtle mineral variation. These are ideas, not final texture requirements.

## Possible forms

The following are candidates for the family, not a locked implementation list:

- Natural Veilstone
- Cracked Veilstone
- Veilstone Bricks
- Chiseled Veilstone
- Slabs, stairs, and walls derived from selected variants

The final palette should be useful for both natural landscapes and player building without producing unnecessary near-duplicate blocks.

## Relationship to materials

Veilstone is **not an ore tier**. The first planned new ore/material tier is Veilcryst; Aetherium remains the final planned new tier. Veilstone may visually surround or contrast with ores, but the names and gameplay roles must remain distinct.

## Open decisions

- Final color palette and texture language
- Which variants are worth implementing
- Where the family appears naturally
- Whether selected variants are used in ancient structures
