# Veilstone — Material Design

> **Documentation status:** This is a provisional design document. The name, appearance, generation, processing, equipment, and mechanics may change. Planned features are not guarantees.

Veilstone is the working name for the **first new material tier** planned for The Veilborn beyond vanilla Diamond. It should help players adapt to the dimension's unusual terrain and environmental phenomena before they reach its more advanced progression.

This page describes gameplay direction rather than fixed stats, recipes, or implementation details.

## 1. Design identity

Veilstone should feel native to The Veilborn: an unfamiliar material that rewards exploration and helps players learn to navigate the dimension.

- **Role:** Early-to-mid progression material focused on traversal and situational environmental utility.
- **Progression position:** Intended to sit above Diamond in selected roles, without making vanilla equipment obsolete.
- **Acquisition:** Primarily exploration and mining, followed by a relatively familiar refining or crafting process.
- **Equipment:** A focused selection of useful items, not necessarily a complete set.
- **Scope:** One of exactly two new ore materials currently planned beyond vanilla Diamond. Aetherium is the other; no third new ore tier is currently planned.

Veilstone should solve specific problems that players encounter in The Veilborn, rather than simply increasing every equipment statistic.

## 2. Appearance and atmosphere

### Visual direction

The current direction is a cool, mineral-like material that feels connected to the dimension's silver oceans, floating islands, and ancient ruins. It should be recognizable in Minecraft's block-based visual style without looking overly bright or magical.

Possible visual cues include:

- Muted stone tones with restrained silver or pale-blue highlights.
- Subtle seams or flecks that distinguish the ore from ordinary stone.
- A refined material with a clearer, more deliberate pattern than the raw ore.
- Crafted equipment with a recognizable silhouette or accent, without excessive glow.

These are visual ideas, not final texture specifications. Veilstone should remain visually distinct from Aetherium, whose current direction is darker, more metallic, and more iridescent.

## 3. Discovery and world placement

Veilstone should be discoverable early enough to support the player's first major period of adaptation to The Veilborn. Finding it should encourage exploration without requiring an advanced boss encounter first.

Potential placement and discovery principles:

- Deposits may be associated with suitable dimension terrain, exposed rock, or selected regions.
- Landmarks and environmental clues may help players recognize promising places to explore.
- Deposits should be uncommon enough to make discovery satisfying, but available reliably enough that progression does not depend on unreasonable luck.
- Mining should feel familiar to Minecraft players; unusual access challenges should come from the surrounding terrain or environment rather than arbitrary requirements.
- The exact biomes, height ranges, vein sizes, generation frequency, and mining requirements remain undecided.

The method of entering The Veilborn remains intentionally undisclosed and is not defined by this document.

## 4. Obtaining and processing

The intended loop is straightforward:

1. **Explore:** Learn the terrain and locate a Veilstone deposit.
2. **Mine:** Collect the ore with an appropriate tool.
3. **Process:** Use a basic, familiar refining or crafting process to obtain usable Veilstone.
4. **Craft:** Choose equipment that supports the player's preferred approach to traversal or environmental challenges.

Processing may require a special ingredient if it adds meaningful discovery, but the process should remain simpler than Aetherium's planned ancient refining station.

### Processing principles

- Keep the basic process easy to understand after discovery.
- Avoid adding unnecessary stations or repetitive steps to the early progression loop.
- Make any special ingredient serve a clear gameplay or exploration purpose.
- Keep exact recipes, processing time, fuel requirements, and tool requirements open until the material's role is better defined.

## 5. Equipment role

Veilstone is intended to provide **a combination of modest movement control and situational environmental utility**. The final item list is not decided.

Potential directions include:

- **Traversal equipment:** Help with controlled movement, aerial handling, falls, or selected gravity anomalies.
- **Environmental utility:** Provide limited help in specific dimension conditions, rather than broad protection against every hazard.
- **Ancient-system interactions:** Allow selected mechanisms to be revealed, accessed, or activated when the player has the relevant item.

These are candidates, not finalized abilities. Not every Veilstone item needs every function, and selected interactions may require one relevant item rather than a full equipment set.

### Ability philosophy

Veilstone should make exploration more manageable without removing its challenge. Movement assistance should reward skill and planning, not turn every gap or gravity anomaly into a non-threat. Environmental utility should be useful in the situations it is designed for, not a universal solution to all hazards.

Equipment may help players interact with selected ancient systems, but the dimension's ruins should not all become inaccessible until a Veilstone item is obtained.

## 6. Balance and progression

Veilstone should represent a meaningful step beyond Diamond in its chosen roles while preserving the value of vanilla gear.

Balance principles:

- Favor useful, situational abilities over large raw-stat increases.
- Give each item a clear purpose that is understandable in play.
- Avoid making a full Veilstone set mandatory for ordinary exploration.
- Ensure traversal assistance does not trivialize the dimension's geography or gravity-related challenges.
- Keep the refining process proportionate to the material's early-to-mid progression role.
- Compare proposed statistics and abilities against relevant vanilla equipment before fixing numbers.

Exact armor values, damage, durability, mining speed, recipes, and ability limits remain open questions.

## 7. Relationship to ancient systems and restoration

Veilstone equipment may help with selected ancient mechanisms. For example, an item could make a hidden feature perceptible or allow a particular mechanism to respond. The precise interactions remain undecided.

These interactions should support a broader progression loop:

- Exploration uncovers structures, clues, and unfamiliar phenomena.
- Veilstone makes selected traversal challenges or interactions more manageable.
- Players reveal clues and activate appropriate mechanisms.
- Smaller discoveries contribute toward larger restoration milestones.
- Restoration can produce lasting changes to the world, with some temporary effects where they create interesting opportunities.

Not every structure needs to require Veilstone, and possessing the material should not automatically explain the dimension's mysteries. These systems should encourage observation and experimentation rather than reduce progression to a checklist of equipment gates.

## 8. Relationship to Aetherium

| Material | Intended role | Processing direction |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond | Vanilla baseline | Vanilla processes |
| **Veilstone** | Early-to-mid progression; traversal, environmental utility, and selected ancient interactions | Basic, familiar process |
| **Aetherium** | Final planned new material tier; advanced specialist equipment | Reusable ancient refining station |

Veilstone and Aetherium should have different identities. Veilstone helps players adapt to The Veilborn; Aetherium represents deeper progression and offers selected advanced equipment options. Aetherium remains the final planned new ore tier, with no third new ore tier currently planned.

## 9. Open questions

- What is the final name and texture direction?
- Which biomes or terrain features should contain Veilstone Ore?
- How common should deposits be, and what mining requirements should apply?
- Is a special processing ingredient necessary, or is a familiar recipe sufficient?
- Which specific traversal and environmental effects are both useful and balanced?
- Which ancient interactions, if any, should require a Veilstone item?
- Which equipment pieces deserve to exist, and what trade-offs should they have?
- How can the material remain valuable without becoming mandatory for every player?

These questions should be answered gradually. This document is a starting point, not a claim that Veilstone or its mechanics have been implemented.

---

**Guiding principle:** Veilstone should help players learn to move through and interact with The Veilborn—an earned, practical tool for exploration, not a universal answer to the dimension's challenges.
